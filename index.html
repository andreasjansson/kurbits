One curve

Every mark is the curve θ(u): a swing, an Euler bend, up to three soft turns and a spiral eye at either end. Up to two limbs grow from it, and smaller copies sprout along everything. A pointed pen swells on down-strokes and thins to a hairline on up-strokes.

Four numbers

x, y and z move through the space; every engine number is a smooth function of them. Detail adds sprouts and generations, and at the high end self-similar, varied miniatures. Aspect widens or heightens an ornament by adding scrolls, never by stretching.

Letters

Every printable ASCII character is a point on the plane z = 1: a trunk and at most two limbs, found by matching the key points, directions and spacing of a drawn letter. Between letters the shapes blend smoothly; away from the plane they dissolve into ornament.

No randomness

The same numbers always draw the same ornament. Counts accept fractions and grow the next element in, so every number sweeps smoothly.
